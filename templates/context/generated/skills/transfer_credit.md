# TransferCredit

**Table:** `helix_silver.transfer_credit` · **Classification:** confidential · **FERPA education record:** yes · **GLBA:** no

Credit earned at another institution and accepted for transfer. Links the external course to the equivalent internal course and tracks how the credit applies.

## Grain and keys

One row per TransferCredit record, keyed by `helix_id`. Required references: `student_ref`.

## Joins

| Column | Joins to |
|--------|----------|
| `student_ref` | `helix_silver.student.helix_id` (Student) |
| `equivalent_course_ref` | `helix_silver.course.helix_id` (Course) |

## Required filters and usage rules

- FERPA education record: access needs legitimate educational interest (99.31(a)(1)). Exclude students with an active directory restriction from anything shared outside the institution (ACM-SPECIAL-001).
- Publish counts only with small-cell suppression (N under 5) per govern/ferpa-suppression-policy.json.
