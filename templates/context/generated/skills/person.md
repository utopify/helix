# Person

**Table:** `helix_silver.person` · **Classification:** confidential · **FERPA education record:** yes · **GLBA:** no

A human being known to the institution in any capacity, student, employee, applicant, alumnus, donor, or contact. The foundational identity resource. Student, Employee, and other role-specific resources reference a Person.

## Grain and keys

One row per Person record, keyed by `helix_id`. Required references: none.

Referenced by: `alumni_profile.person_ref`, `award_honor.person_ref`, `constituent.person_ref`, `continuing_education.person_ref`, `employee.person_ref`, `employment_outcome.person_ref`, `first_destination_survey.person_ref`, `licensure.person_ref`, `student.person_ref`

## Required filters and usage rules

- FERPA education record: access needs legitimate educational interest (99.31(a)(1)). Exclude students with an active directory restriction from anything shared outside the institution (ACM-SPECIAL-001).
- Publish counts only with small-cell suppression (N under 5) per govern/ferpa-suppression-policy.json.

## Column rules

- `identifiers[].value (where identifiers[].type in national_id, ssn_last4, passport, drivers_license)`: restricted. Only helix_analyst_restricted sees cleartext. Silver engineers see SHA-256 tokens for identity resolution. All others see 'RESTRICTED'. HELIX Person carries SSN and national IDs inside identifiers[]; bank and routing numbers are not Person attributes. If your institution extends Person with them,...
- `name.family`, `name.given`, `name.middle`, `name.preferred_first`, `name.preferred_last`, `birth_date`, `contact.emails[].address`, `contact.phones[].number`, `contact.addresses[]`, `emergency_contacts[]`: confidential. helix_analyst_confidential and helix_steward see cleartext. helix_analyst_standard sees partial mask (first initial, domain-only email). helix_research sees opaque token.
