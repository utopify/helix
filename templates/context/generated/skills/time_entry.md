# TimeEntry

**Table:** `helix_silver.time_entry` · **Classification:** confidential · **FERPA education record:** no · **GLBA:** no

A time entry, reported hours for a non-exempt employee or student worker, with worktag/chartfield distribution and approval status.

## Grain and keys

One row per TimeEntry record, keyed by `helix_id`. Required references: `employee_ref`.

## Joins

| Column | Joins to |
|--------|----------|
| `employee_ref` | `helix_silver.employee.helix_id` (Employee) |
| `grant_ref` | `helix_silver.grant.helix_id` (Grant) |
