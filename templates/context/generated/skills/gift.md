# Gift

**Table:** `helix_silver.gift` · **Classification:** confidential · **FERPA education record:** no · **GLBA:** no

A philanthropic transaction: gift, pledge, pledge payment, matching gift, planned gift, or in-kind donation to the institution.

## Grain and keys

One row per Gift record, keyed by `helix_id`. Required references: `constituent_ref`.

## Joins

| Column | Joins to |
|--------|----------|
| `constituent_ref` | `helix_silver.constituent.helix_id` (Constituent) |
| `campaign_ref` | `helix_silver.campaign.helix_id` (Campaign) |
| `pledge_ref` | `helix_silver.gift.helix_id` (Gift) |

Referenced by: `engagement_activity.linked_gift_ref`

## Required filters and usage rules

- Don't add pledge payments to pledges in the same total (CASE rules)
- Follow the classification above for any output.

## Column rules

- `constituent_ref (where anonymous_flag = true)`, `soft_credit_constituents (where anonymous_flag = true)`: confidential. Anonymous gifts: donor identity is masked for every role except helix_analyst_confidential holding advancement steward authorization. Gift amounts roll up to segments for helix_analyst_standard.
