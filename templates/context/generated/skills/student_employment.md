# StudentEmployment

**Table:** `helix_silver.student_employment` · **Classification:** confidential · **FERPA education record:** yes · **GLBA:** no

Federal Work-Study (FWS) or other student employment for an aid year: award, earnings tracking, community-service participation, job placement, and federal/institutional cost share. FWS/FISAP context. Classified confidential (FERPA education record).

## Grain and keys

One row per StudentEmployment record, keyed by `helix_id`. Required references: `student_ref`.

## Joins

| Column | Joins to |
|--------|----------|
| `student_ref` | `helix_silver.student.helix_id` (Student) |
| `supervisor_ref` | `helix_silver.employee.helix_id` (Employee) |

## Required filters and usage rules

- FERPA education record: access needs legitimate educational interest (99.31(a)(1)). Exclude students with an active directory restriction from anything shared outside the institution (ACM-SPECIAL-001).
- Publish counts only with small-cell suppression (N under 5) per govern/ferpa-suppression-policy.json.

## Column rules

- `hourly_rate`, `earnings_ytd`, `fws_earnings_to_date`, `fws_award_amount`: restricted (pay). Student pay follows HR pay rules. Cleartext for helix_analyst_restricted (finaid or hr subtype). Supervisors and confidential analysts see job details but 'RESTRICTED' for pay.
