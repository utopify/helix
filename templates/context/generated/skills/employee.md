# Employee

**Table:** `helix_silver.employee` · **Classification:** confidential · **FERPA education record:** no · **GLBA:** no

An employee / worker, the core HR identity. Links to a HELIX Person (a single human may be both Employee and Student). Carries hire/termination, worker type, FTE, FLSA, citizenship/visa, and contact info. SSN/national ID tokenized at silver.

## Grain and keys

One row per Employee record, keyed by `helix_id`. Required references: `person_ref`.

## Column rules

- `national_id`, `date_of_birth`, `home_address`: restricted. Only helix_analyst_restricted_hr sees cleartext. All others see SHA-256 token. Bank and routing numbers are not HELIX Employee attributes; they stay in the payroll system of record.
