# EngagementActivity

**Table:** `helix_silver.engagement_activity` · **Classification:** internal · **FERPA education record:** no · **GLBA:** no

A trackable interaction between a constituent and the institution: event attendance, email engagement, volunteer activity, social media interaction, campus visit, or meeting with a development officer.

## Grain and keys

One row per EngagementActivity record, keyed by `helix_id`. Required references: `constituent_ref`.
