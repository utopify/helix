# EmploymentOutcome

**Table:** `helix_silver.employment_outcome` · **Classification:** confidential · **FERPA education record:** yes · **GLBA:** no

A first-destination or ongoing employment outcome for a graduate. Aligned with the NACE (National Association of Colleges and Employers) First-Destination Survey Standards, which define the standard for reporting career outcomes of graduating classes. Captures where and how a graduate is employed, salary, and relationship to the field of study. First-destination data is typically collected at or within six months of graduation.

## Grain and keys

One row per EmploymentOutcome record, keyed by `helix_id`. Required references: `student_ref`.

## Required filters and usage rules

- FERPA education record: access needs legitimate educational interest (99.31(a)(1)). Exclude students with an active directory restriction from anything shared outside the institution (ACM-SPECIAL-001).
- Publish counts only with small-cell suppression (N under 5) per govern/ferpa-suppression-policy.json.

## Column rules

- `salary_amount`, `signing_bonus`: confidential (salary). Individual salary is visible to helix_analyst_confidential with a career-services purpose and to helix_analyst_restricted. helix_research sees salary bands. Published only as suppressed aggregates (median by program with N of at least 5).
- `employer_name`, `job_title`, `employment_location_city`: confidential (re-identifying). With graduation year and program these re-identify a graduate. helix_research and helix_analyst_standard see masked values.
