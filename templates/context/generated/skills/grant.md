# Grant

**Table:** `helix_silver.grant` · **Classification:** internal · **FERPA education record:** no · **GLBA:** no

A sponsored program / grant award, the full lifecycle record for research and other externally funded awards. Carries sponsor, PI, CFDA, budget (direct/indirect), F&A rate, and cost sharing. Subject to 2 CFR 200 (Uniform Guidance) for federal awards.

## Grain and keys

One row per Grant record, keyed by `helix_id`. Required references: none.

Referenced by: `asset.grant_ref`, `expense_report.grant_ref`, `payroll_result.grant_ref`, `time_entry.grant_ref`
