# Requisition

**Table:** `helix_silver.requisition` · **Classification:** confidential · **FERPA education record:** no · **GLBA:** no

A job requisition, a position opening in recruiting, with applicant pipeline, disposition, and EEO data. Candidate PII is confidential; EEO data kept separate from hiring decisions.

## Grain and keys

One row per Requisition record, keyed by `helix_id`. Required references: none.

Referenced by: `purchase_order.requisition_ref`
