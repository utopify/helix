# Constituent

**Table:** `helix_silver.constituent` · **Classification:** confidential · **FERPA education record:** no · **GLBA:** no

A person or organization with a relationship to the institution for advancement purposes: alumni, donors, parents, friends, corporate partners, foundations. Extends Person with advancement-specific attributes.

## Grain and keys

One row per Constituent record, keyed by `helix_id`. Required references: none.

## Joins

| Column | Joins to |
|--------|----------|
| `person_ref` | `helix_silver.person.helix_id` (Person) |
| `student_ref` | `helix_silver.student.helix_id` (Student) |

Referenced by: `alumni_profile.constituent_ref`, `engagement_activity.constituent_ref`, `gift.constituent_ref`

## Required filters and usage rules

- Exclude status do_not_solicit and do_not_contact from any outreach list
- Follow the classification above for any output.

## Column rules

- `giving_capacity.estimated_capacity`, `giving_capacity.capacity_rating`, `giving_capacity.screening_date`, `giving_capacity.screening_source`, `engagement_score`: confidential. Wealth screening and prospect ratings. Only helix_analyst_confidential with advancement steward authorization. helix_analyst_standard sees aggregate giving by segment only. Constituent names and contact details come from Person and follow the Person PII rule.
